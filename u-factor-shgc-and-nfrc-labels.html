Reading NFRC Window Labels: U-Factor, SHGC and Visible Transmittance

The white NFRC sticker measures whole-window performance—frame, spacer, and glass together—not just center-of-glass lab claims.

NFRC metricWhat it measuresHow to read it
U-FactorRate of non-solar heat loss through the whole windowLower is better insulation in every climate
Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC)Fraction of solar heat passing through glassLower cuts summer air-conditioning load; cold northern zones sometimes allow slightly higher SHGC on south faces
Visible Transmittance (VT)Share of natural daylight passing throughHigher brings brighter interiors; very low-SHGC tints reduce VT slightly
Air Leakage (AL)Cubic feet of air passing per square foot of window areaLower indicates tighter weatherstripping against drafts
