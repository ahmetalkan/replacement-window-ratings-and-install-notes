Pocket Insert vs Full-Frame Window Replacement

Two quotes for the exact same window brand can differ sharply because one contractor plans a pocket insert while the other plans a full-frame tear-out down to rough framing studs.

AspectPocket (insert) replacementFull-frame replacement
What staysExisting exterior frame, interior casing trim, and siding remain undisturbedOld frame removed down to rough opening studs
Hidden rot & air sealingCannot inspect hidden framing or uninsulated old sash-weight cavitiesAllows sill-pan flashing, rot repair, and continuous perimeter insulation
Glass viewing areaSlightly reduced because new frame sits inside old framePreserves full original daylight opening
Typical fitSquare, dry, rot-free frames with intact original interior woodworkSoft/rotten sills, leaking exterior flashing, or changing window style/size
