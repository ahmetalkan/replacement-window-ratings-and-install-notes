Vinyl, Fiberglass and Wood-Clad Window Frames Compared

Glass packages from different brands are often nearly identical; the frame material largely drives cost, sightline thickness, paintability, and expansion behavior in sun and cold.

  • Vinyl (PVC): Widely available and budget-friendly with multi-chambered insulating extrusions and low upkeep; expands and contracts more with temperature swings than glass, so dark exterior vinyl in intense sun requires heat-reflective formulations and reinforced sashes.
  • Pultruded fiberglass: Very low thermal expansion close to glass itself, strong thin profiles, and paintable surfaces—helping seals hold steady over decades of hot-cold cycles.
  • Aluminum-clad wood: Warm stainable or paintable wood interior paired with a weather-resistant exterior metal cladding; heavier cost and requires careful sill flashing to keep exterior water out of interior wood.
